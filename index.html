Dungeon Reclaim

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

The short version

Dungeon Reclaim is a single-player game. We do not ask for your name, email or any account, and we do not run our own servers. Your progress is saved only on your device. If the game shows ads, the ad provider (Google AdMob) may collect some device data, as described below.

Who we are

Dungeon Reclaim (Android package com.aidentif.dungeonreclaim) is developed and published by Tif Aiden, an independent developer ("we", "us"). You can contact us at ltif427@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

  • Game progress (heroes, items, dungeons cleared, currencies, achievements) is saved locally on your device so you can continue playing.
  • Settings (language, sound, music, vibration and notification preferences) are saved locally.

This information never leaves your device through us. Uninstalling the game, or using Settings → Reset Progress in the game, deletes it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example, when a daily trial is about to close or your heroes have gathered loot). They are created on the device itself; no push server and no personal data are involved. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your phone's system settings.

Advertising

The game may show ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and general device information (model, operating system, language);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • interactions with ads (views, clicks).

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or limit ad personalisation at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads on some devices).

In-app purchases

If the game offers in-app purchases, payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made so the item can be delivered.

What we do not do

  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Children

Dungeon Reclaim is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we hold no personal data on our side, most requests are covered by deleting the game's local data on your device. For data collected by Google AdMob, use Google's tools linked above. For anything else, email us and we will reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes (for example, if new features are added). The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's update notes.
